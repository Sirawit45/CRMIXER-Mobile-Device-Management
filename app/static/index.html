Connect your Android phone via USB cable or Wi-Fi to start controlling and mirroring screens in real-time.

Quick Setup Steps:
1. Go to Settings → Developer options on your phone.
2. Enable USB debugging (and USB debugging (Security settings) if available).
3. Plug your phone into PC via USB cable.
4. When prompted on phone, tap "Always allow from this computer".
5. Click the Refresh button at the top!
